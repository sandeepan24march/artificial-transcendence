01 / THE CONCEPT

What could exist
beyond intelligence?

Artificial Transcendence is a forward-looking idea, not an established scientific classification. It asks what happens when measuring machines against human intelligence is no longer a useful benchmark.

It imagines systems capable of discovering new forms of reasoning, creating knowledge beyond current human understanding, and operating across scientific and cognitive domains that have yet to be defined.
