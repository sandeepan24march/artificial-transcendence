Superintelligence.

A hypothetical stage in which machine intelligence substantially exceeds human intellectual capability across a wide range of cognitive domains.

THE CONCEPT

What is ASI?

Artificial Superintelligence, or ASI, describes a hypothetical form of machine intelligence that would outperform humans across most or all economically and intellectually important cognitive tasks.

ASI is currently a theoretical concept rather than an established technological category. Its implications depend heavily on assumptions about intelligence, autonomy, learning, control and the ability of systems to improve themselves.

INTELLIGENCE ≠ WISDOM

More intelligence does not
automatically mean better judgment.

A system could become extraordinarily capable at reasoning, planning or discovery without necessarily possessing human values, wisdom, empathy or moral understanding.

This distinction makes alignment, interpretability, robustness, security and governance central questions in any discussion of advanced machine intelligence.
