THE FOUNDATION

What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence is the field of creating machine-based systems capable of performing tasks that involve forms of perception, learning, reasoning, decision-making, language understanding and planning.

Modern AI is not a single technology. It is an ecosystem of techniques and architectures ranging from traditional machine learning and deep neural networks to foundation models, generative AI, multimodal systems and increasingly autonomous AI agents.

THE EVOLUTION

From narrow systems
toward general intelligence.

01

Machine Learning

Systems learn patterns from data instead of relying entirely on explicitly programmed rules.

02

Deep Learning

Large neural networks learn increasingly complex representations from massive datasets.

03

Generative AI

Models capable of generating text, images, audio, video, software and other forms of content.

04

Multimodal AI

Systems capable of working across multiple forms of information such as text, images, audio and video.

05

Agentic AI

Systems that can interpret goals, plan actions, use tools and execute multi-step tasks.

06
